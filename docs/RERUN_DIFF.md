## In plain words: did any verdict or headline claim change?

**No verdict label changed.** C1 refuted, C2 held, C3 held (by construction), C4 undetermined, C5 held, C6 not applicable (T2 = 25 µs variant undetermined), O4 a measurement: all as before.

**Three headline claims changed, and the text was rewritten to match:**

1. **F2, superconducting qubit: "single-qubit readout tuning is enough" no longer holds for the bit-flip memory.** The code's best readout time at d = 3 (hard) is now 0.729 µs [0.697, 0.844], wholly below the single-qubit 0.910 µs, and pL(0.9 µs) − pL(0.75 µs) is resolved above zero at d = 3 and d = 5 (before: 0.710 µs [0.662, 0.990], not resolved). Intervals below τ*_phys at d = 3 and 5: 4 of 8 (before 1 of 8). The phase-flip memory is unchanged (shift not resolved). C1 stays **refuted** because the pre-registered statement needed the shift in both memories. For the ion the claim still holds (23.5 µs [17.3, 25.1] contains 23.0 µs).
2. **F1 at d = 5: the ion's d = 5 best readout time is no longer resolved** (27.4 µs, 100% of bootstrap resamples tied). The held-out numbers replicate (first decoder 0.090%, learned 0.021%, 4.2×; suppression 15×; "13 of 26 → 0 of 26" unchanged), but they are now described as read at the lowest-error grid point, not at a resolved best readout time. Those two rows drop out of C5, which now rests on 12 resolved rows (0 learned above naive, 11 below; out of sample only at d = 3).
3. **Ablation: "once the diagonal edges are in, learned rates add nothing we can resolve" now holds at d = 5 only.** At d = 3 the learned decoder (0.32% hard, 0.31% soft) lies below the uniform-rate intervals; rates-only at d = 5 now gives a small resolved change (0.097% → 0.086%).

**Smaller changes:** in-sample "naive soft worse than hard at 13 of 39" is now 9 of 39 (paired 18 → 9); O4 now has 4 of 5 intervals containing 1 (d5 r5: 1.048 [1.013, 1.084]); C6 variant: X shorter in 3 of 4 point estimates (before 4 of 4); trade-off ratios "about 3800×" → about 3700×, "about twice as accurate" → about 1.8×; the superconducting T1 × 2 sensitivity is no longer resolved; V6 now shows no L0/L1 difference (before 1 of 48). The trapped-ion budget ratios (≈500× and ≈7000×) and the crosstalk budget (optimum resolved below τ*_phys only at 1e-3 /µs, ≈60× the sourced rate) are unchanged in substance.

**Not changed by design:** V9/V9L fingerprint `53933f98` (it decodes the first 1000 shots of `rep_d3_r3_L0`, which are the all-zero outcome in both the old and the new bank).
